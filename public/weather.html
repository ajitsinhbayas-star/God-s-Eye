Live 3D Earth Weather Radar, Atmosphere & Meteorological Command Center

God's Eye provides real-time global Doppler radar mosaics, precipitation rate calculations, and convective storm tracking on an interactive 3D WebGL globe. Ingesting open data from RainViewer API, Open-Meteo numerical weather prediction models (ECMWF, GFS, ICON), and NASA FIRMS active fire hotspots.

Key Meteorological Features:

  • Multi-band Doppler radar precipitation analysis with millimeter-per-hour and dBZ reflectivity measurements.
  • Full-time bright daylight illumination across all longitudes without dark shadow obscuration.
  • Google Earth-style Level-of-Detail (LOD) place labeling featuring 132 major world cities and regional weather hubs.
  • Active tropical cyclone tracking, central pressure estimations, and forecast cones (IMD, NOAA NHC, JTWC).
  • NASA FIRMS VIIRS and MODIS thermal fire anomaly detection.
